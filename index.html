FAQ

WordPress hosting questions

How much does WordPress hosting cost?+

Solo starts from 249/month, Freelancer from 379/month, and Startup is৳499/month on the 11-month billing cycle. All plans include 5% VAT, LiteSpeed caching, WP Toolkit, and free SSL.

What is the minimum billing duration, and can I choose other cycles?+

The minimum billing period is 6 months. You can choose between 6 months, 11 months, 12 months, 24 months, 26 months, or 48 months. All 12+ month commitments include a free .COM domain for the first year, with 5% VAT calculated transparently.

Which plan should I choose?+

Solo fits a blog or a few small sites. Freelancer adds daily backups, malware scanning and a CDN for client work. Startup adds staging, Git, a web application firewall and DDoS protection for agencies and stores.

How does the free .com domain work?+

Choose any plan on the 1-year term and add a .com domain at checkout. The domain is free for its first year.

Which plans include daily backups?+

Freelancer and Startup include automated daily backups. Solo includes 5 GB of extra backup storage.

Is the migration really free?+

Yes, on every plan. Our team moves your WordPress files and database, you check the site, then you point your domain to Softcreators.

Can I run WooCommerce?+

Yes. WooCommerce runs on every plan, and Startup adds staging and a firewall for stores. If you want us to manage the store for you, see Managed WooCommerce.

Where are the servers located?+

Your site runs on servers in a European Union data center. On Freelancer and Startup, the CDN serves static files from locations closer to your visitors.

How fast is it for visitors in Bangladesh and South Asia?+

Your site runs in a European Union data center; on Freelancer and Startup, the CDN serves static files from locations closer to your visitors, which hides most of the distance for images, scripts and styles. If almost all of your visitors are in Bangladesh, see BDIX web hosting; if you need a server in Asia, see VPS hosting.

Is WordPress installed with one click?+

Yes. WordPress comes pre-installed on WordPress Hosting, so you start from the dashboard rather than from an installer. Our one-click WordPress install guide explains what an installer sets up and compares this plan with Managed WordPress.

Is this WordPress hosting for Finland (WordPress hosting Suomi)?+

These plans run in a European Union data center with a CDN on Freelancer and Startup, so Finnish visitors are served from nearby. Best WordPress hosting in Finland compares the options; for a server located in Finland itself, choose a Finland VPS.

Can I pay from Bangladesh, India or outside the EU, in my own currency?+

Yes. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards through Stripe, PayPal, and the bKash and Nagad mobile wallets for customers in Bangladesh. Switch the currency in the header to US dollars, euros or Bangladeshi taka and the price you see is the price you pay at checkout. See payment methods and how billing works.

How do I reach support, and where are the company details?+

Company details are on the about us and registrar information pages. Live chat is open 24/7, support tickets are answered from your dashboard, and Phone and phone support run Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 Bangladesh time (GMT+6). See get support.
